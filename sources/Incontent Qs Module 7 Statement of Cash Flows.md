##### Lesson 1: Statement of Cash Flows for an Individual Company



The statement of cash flows should only present the following items: (1) Cash flows from operating activities, (2) Cash flows from investing activities, (3) Cash flows from financing activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is b (false). 



&#x20;



While it is true that the cash flows from each of the activities listed here are presented in the statement of cash flows, they are not the only items presented. The statement of cash flows should present the following headings and totals/sub-totals: 



Cash flows from operating activities



Net cash from/(used in) operating activities



Cash flows from investing activities



Net cash from/(used in) investing activities



Cash flows from financing activities



Net cash from/(used in) financing activities



Net increase in cash and cash equivalents



Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of the year



Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year



Cash inflows from issuing company shares are presented under operating activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is b (false).



Cash inflows from issuing shares are presented under financing activities, as they are related to the entity's capital financing.



Proceeds from the sale of property, plant and equipment (PPE) are presented under investing activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is a (true).



Proceeds from the sale of long-term assets such as property, plant and equipment are considered investing activities, as they relate to the investment of capital in these assets.



Cash outflows from acquiring equity or debt instruments of other entities are presented under operating activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is b (false).



Such cash outflows are presented under investing activities, as they represent investments in other entities.



Cash paid to suppliers for goods and services is presented under investing activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is b (false).







Cash outflows to suppliers for goods and services are presented under operating activities, as they are part of the entity's primary revenue-producing operations.



Cash receipts from the sale of goods and services are presented under investing activities.





a) True





b) False



The correct answer is b (false).



Cash receipts from the sale of goods and services are a part of operating activities as they arise from the primary revenue-producing activities of the entity.

##### Lesson 2: Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows







Omega Ltd (Omega) holds a 90% stake in Zeta Ltd (Zeta). During the financial year, the following transactions occurred between Omega and Zeta. 



Zeta acquired a building from Omega for £1m in cash. 



Zeta distributed dividends amounting to £500,000.



Which of the following transactions should be included in the consolidated statement of cash flows?





a) Zeta acquiring £1m building from Omega for £1m in cash.





b) Zeta paying £450,000 dividends to Omega.





c) Zeta paying £50,000 dividends to NCI.



The correct answer is c.



Only cash flows outside the group are presented in the consolidated statement of cash flows and therefore only dividends paid to the NCI are presented.



Anemos Ltd (Anemos) holds a 30% stake in Volt Ltd (Volt), an associate, and a 50% stake in Watt Ltd (Watt), a joint venture. During the financial year, several transactions and events occurred:



Anemos recognised its share of Volt profit, amounting to £200,000.



Anemos received dividends of £50,000 from Volt.



Anemos engaged in cash sales transactions worth £100,000 with Watt.



Watt paid dividends of £40,000 to Anemos.



Based on Anemos’ investments and transactions, which of the following should be included in its consolidated statement of cash flows under the IFRS framework?





a) Only the dividends received from Volt and the cash sales transactions with Watt.





b) Dividends received from Volt and Watt, and the cash sales transactions with Watt.





c) The share of profit from Volt, dividends from both Volt and Watt, and cash transactions with Watt. 





d) Only the dividends received from both Volt and Watt.



The correct answer is b. 







In the consolidated statement of cash flows, only actual cash transactions are included.



This includes dividends received from both the associate (Volt) and the joint venture (Watt) as they represent actual cash inflows to Anemos.







Additionally, cash transactions with the joint venture (Watt), such as the cash sales, are included as they are actual transactions between Anemos and Watt.







The share of profit recognised from Volt is not a cash transaction and thus is not included in the consolidated statement of cash flows.
