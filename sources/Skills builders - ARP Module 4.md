Question 2		0 / 1 point

On 1 January 20X2, Knowles Ltd granted its sales director 10,000 share options. These will vest on 31 December 20X4 provided that the director remained employed and the company had sales growth averaging 5% per annum. The fair value of a share option at 1 January 20X2 was £6.60 and at 31 December 20X2 it was £9. 



At 31 December 20X2 the sales director remained employed, but the sales growth was only 4% in the year.



Which of the following options correctly describes the correct accounting treatment in the year ended 31 December 20X2? 



Correct AnswerUnselected

a) 



No accounting entries are made in respect of the share options



Unselected

An expense of £22,000 is recognised



Unselected

c) 



A credit is made to retained earnings of £30,000



Unselected

d) 



A fair value remeasurement of £414,000 is recognised in profit or loss





Hide question 2 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is a.



The requirement to remain employed is a service vesting condition and the sales growth requirement is a performance condition unrelated to share price. Both of these vesting conditions are reflected in the measurement of the share-based payment.



Since the growth target is not met, at 31 December 20X2 the share options are not expected to vest and therefore no accounting entries are made.

Question 9		0 / 1 point

The directors of Everett Ltd (Everett) would like advice on accounting for their new employee SARs scheme which requires employees to work for a period of 3 years. Which statement provides correct advice? 



Unselected

a) 



This is an equity-settled share-based payment transaction and it should be measured at the fair value on the grant date of the instruments granted



Unselected

b) 



At the grant date Everett should recognise a liability, presented as other payables



Correct AnswerUnselected

c) 



This is a cash-settled share-based payment transaction and the carrying amount of the liability at the reporting date should be separately disclosed



Unselected

d) 



The requirement for employees to work for 3 years is a performance vesting condition and must be reflected in the measurement of the transaction





Hide question 9 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is c.



This is an example of a cash-settled share-based payment transaction. 

It is measured at the fair value of the SARs issued and the fair value is remeasured at each reporting date. 

Everett should recognise a liability; however, until service has been provided by employees there is no liability. Therefore, no liability is recognised at the grant date. 

The requirement for employees to work for 3 years is a service vesting condition and must be reflected in the measurement of the transaction.
