Question 3		0 / 1 point

Toronto Ltd (Toronto) borrowed £500,000 from an unrelated company on 6 May 20X5 and has agreed to repay this amount in the future by issuing 200,000 of its own shares.



What financial instrument should Toronto recognise in its financial statements?



Unselected

a) 



A financial asset



Unselected

b) 



A financial liability



Correct AnswerUnselected

c) 



An equity instrument



Unselected

d) 



A compound instrument





Hide question 3 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is c.



The loan is not a financial asset because Toronto has borrowed rather than lent money.



The loan does not meet the definition of a financial liability because there is no contractual obligation to deliver cash and Toronto is obliged to deliver a fixed rather than a variable number of equity shares.



Since the loan does not have the characteristics of a financial liability, it cannot be a compound instrument.



The loan is an equity instrument because the lender will obtain a residual interest in the assets of Toronto when the loan is repaid.

Question 5		0 / 1 point

Halifax Ltd (Halifax) acquired an investment property during the year ended 31 December 20X5 which is measured using the fair value model. This was funded by way of a fixed-term bank loan. Halifax is required to pay interest on a six-monthly basis at a variable rate.



Which statement is correct? 



Unselected

a) 



The loan must be classified as measured at FVTPL.



Unselected

b) 



The loan must be classified as measured at amortised cost.



Correct AnswerUnselected

c) 



The loan may be classified as measured at either FVTPL or amortised cost.



Unselected

d) 



The loan may be classified as measured at either FVTOCI or amortised cost.





Hide question 5 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is c. 



Ordinarily, the loan would be classified as measured at amortised cost, however in this case it may be designated as measured at FVTPL in order to avoid an accounting mismatch. If this designation were made, changes in the fair value of the loan would be matched to changes in the fair value of the property in the statement of profit or loss. 



Financial liabilities are never measured at FVTOCI.
