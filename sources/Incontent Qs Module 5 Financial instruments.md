Lesson 1: Presentation of Financial Instruments



Which of the following should be classified as a financial liability? Select all that apply.





a) An investment in the equity shares of another company that has fallen in value since it was acquired





b) An amount payable to HMRC in respect of corporation tax





c) An issue of loan notes (debentures)





d) Deferred income recognised in respect of a contract with a customer





e) A trade payable



The correct answers are c and e.



&#x20;



An investment in equity shares is a financial asset, regardless of whether it has fallen in value since acquisition.



&#x20;



A tax liability is not a financial liability because it is a statutory rather than a contractual obligation.



&#x20;



An issue of loan notes (also known as debentures or loan stock) is the equivalent of a company taking a loan. Therefore, this is a financial liability.



&#x20;



Deferred income is not a financial liability because the contractual obligation is to deliver goods or services to the customer, not cash or a financial asset.



&#x20;



A trade payable is a financial liability because there is a contractual obligation to deliver cash to the supplier of goods or services.



On 1 November 20X4 Aruba Ltd (Aruba) entered into a forward contract to buy $500,000 USD on 31 January 20X5 at an exchange rate of £1:$1.25. There was no cost to Aruba to enter the contract. At Aruba’s reporting date of 31 December 20X4, the same contract for settlement on 31 January 20X5 would attract an exchange rate of £1: $1.28.



&#x20;



Which of the following best describes the financial instrument held by Aruba?





a) It is a primary financial instrument classified as a financial asset.





b) It is a primary financial instrument classified as a financial liability.





c) It is a derivative financial instrument classified as a financial asset.





d) It is a derivative financial instrument classified as a financial liability.



The correct answer is d.



&#x20;



The forward contract is a derivative because:



its value to Aruba changes in response to movements in the Sterling-Dollar exchange rate

it had no initial cost

it will be settled in the future, on 31 January 20X5.

Aruba will pay £400,000 ($500,000/1.25) to obtain the Dollars. If it entered into the contract at the reporting date it would pay £390,625 ($500,000/1.28). The contract is therefore ‘out of the money’, ie it requires Aruba to pay more to obtain the $500,000 than it would have to pay if it entered a contract at the reporting date. The exchange rate has moved against Aruba and this is a financial liability.



Which of the following should be classified as a financial asset of Tobago Ltd?





a)A contract to buy gold at a fixed price (the contract price is lower than the current market price of gold; the contract will result in a delivery of gold)





b) An investment in a subsidiary in the consolidated financial statements





c) An option to acquire equity shares in another company for less than market price





d) A rent pre-payment



The correct answer is c.



&#x20;



Although the contract to buy gold is ‘in the money’, it is not a financial instrument because it provides a contractual right to exchange cash for gold rather than a contractual right to exchange financial instruments.



&#x20;



The investment in a subsidiary is not recognised as an asset in the consolidated financial statements; instead, its assets, liabilities, income and expenses are consolidated.



&#x20;



An option to acquire equity shares in another company for less than the market price is a financial asset because it is a contract to exchange financial instruments under favourable conditions.



&#x20;



A rent prepayment is not a financial asset because it provides a contractual right to receive a service rather than a right to receive a financial asset.

&#x20;



Which of the following statements correctly describes how the presentation requirements of IAS 32 are applied to the shares?





a) The shares are presented as an equity instrument and the dividends paid are presented in the statement of changes in equity.





b) The shares are presented as an equity instrument and the dividends paid are presented as an interest expense in the statement of profit or loss.





c) The shares are presented as a financial liability and the dividends paid are presented in the statement of changes in equity.





d) The shares are presented as a financial liability and the dividends paid are presented as an interest expense in the statement of profit or loss.



Correct

The correct answer is d.

##### Lesson 4: Financial Assets: Recognition and Classification





Which of the following financial assets meet the SPPI test? Select all options that apply.





a) An investment in equity shares that pay regular dividends





b) An investment in fixed-term debentures that pays a variable rate of interest linked to SONIA (Sterling Over Night Index Average)





c) An option to buy $100,000 at a preferential rate



The correct answer is b.



&#x20;



There are no contractual cash flows associated with equity shares and therefore they cannot meet the SPPI test.



&#x20;



The debentures give rise to interest and repayment cash flows. These are contractual and arise on specific dates. The interest rate is variable; however, this does not affect the fact that interest cash flows are consideration for the time value of money and credit risk associated with the principal outstanding.



&#x20;



A derivative does not give rise to contractual cash flows on specific dates.



What is the correct measurement classification of the following financial assets?



An investment in debentures that pay interest at a fixed rate and are expected to be held to redemption



An investment in equity shares



A forward contract to buy gold that will be settled net in cash on a fixed date and is ‘in the money’



An investment in loan notes with fixed rate interest and fixed term redemption that is held for trading purposes





a) 1 and 2 – FVTOCI; 3 – FVTPL; 4 – amortised cost





b) 1 and 4 – amortised cost; 2 – FVTPL; 3 – FVTOCI





c) 1 – amortised cost; 2, 3 and 4 – FVTPL





d) 1 – amortised cost; 2 and 3 – FVTPL; 4 – FVTOCI



The correct answer is c.



&#x20;



The investment in debentures meets the SPPI test and is managed within a ‘held to collect’ business model. Therefore, it is measured at amortised cost.

The investment in equity shares does not meet the SPPI test. Therefore, it is measured at FVTPL.

The forward contract does not meet the SPPI test. Therefore, it is measured at FVTPL.

The investment in loan notes meets the SPPI test. It is not managed within a ‘held to collect’ or a ‘held to collect and sell’ business model. Therefore, it is measured at FVTPL.

##### Lesson 7: Financial Assets: Derecognition



Barbuda Ltd (Barbuda) factors its trade receivables balances to Factor Co. Factor Co pays 90% of the balances on a non-recourse basis, meaning that it cannot transfer debts that are late or bad back to Barbuda. Title to the receivables is transferred on a weekly basis. 



&#x20;



Should Barbuda derecognise its trade receivables at the end of each week?





a) Yes





b) No



The correct answer is a.



&#x20;



The contractual rights to receive cash from receivables have been transferred; however, the assets are only derecognised if substantially all of the risks and rewards associated with the receivables are also transferred.



&#x20;



The transfer is on a non-recourse basis, meaning that the factor cannot demand repayment if receivables do not pay, and Barbuda does not face the risk of having to reimburse the factor. Equally, Barbuda will not benefit from the receipt of cash from receivables. 



&#x20;



Therefore, Barbuda’s exposure to variability in the amounts and timing of cash flows is not significant in relation to total variability. This indicates that risks and rewards have been transferred and the receivables should be derecognised.

##### Lesson 9: Financial Instruments: Disclosures

The correct answer is a.

Carrying amounts of financial assets by classification can be disclosed on the face of the SOFP or in the notes to the financial statements.

The carrying amount of FVTPL financial assets must be disclosed separately if they are designated as FVTPL rather than mandatorily FVTPL.

There is no requirement to disclose the amortised cost carrying amount of financial assets that are measured at fair value.
