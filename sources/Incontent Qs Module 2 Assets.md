##### Lesson 3: Investment Property



Which of the following meet the definition of investment property? Select all that apply.





a) Land held for an undetermined future use





b) A building that is vacant but is held to be leased out under operating leases





c) Land on which a new administrative building will be built in the future





d) A property that is under construction but will be held for capital appreciation when complete



The correct answers are a, b and d.



Land held for an undetermined future use is assumed to be held for capital appreciation.

A building that is leased out under an operating lease, or a building that is currently vacant but that is held to be leased out under an operating lease, is investment property.

Land on which a new administrative building will be built in the future is owner-occupied.

A property that is under development to be used as investment property in the future meets the definition now.



Nelson Ltd (Nelson) owns an apartment block in Stirling. At 31 December 20X7 ten of the 20 apartments in the block are empty, but are available to rent to third parties under operating leases, and ten are rented to Nelson employees, all of whom pay market rents. Nelson organises and funds window cleaning services for the apartment block and also provides maintenance services for communal areas.



Which of the following statements is correct?





a) IAS 40 should be applied to account for half of the property and IAS 16 should be applied to account for the other half of the property





b) IAS 40 should be applied to the whole property





c) IAS 16 should be applied to the whole property



The correct answer is a.







Ten of the 20 apartments are empty but are available to rent to third parties under operating leases, therefore they meet the definition of investment property (IAS 40). The ancillary services provided are not significant and so do not affect this classification.







Ten of the 20 apartments are rented to Nelson employees, therefore they meet the definition of owner-occupied property (IAS 16).







The parts could be sold separately and therefore they are ‘split-accounted’, ie IAS 16 applies to half and IAS 40 to half.

How many CGUs are there in Sharman?
