Question 4		0 / 1 point

Which of the three main frameworks of sustainability reporting require reporting entities to consider the impact of the business on the economy, environment and society in preparing disclosures? 



Unselected

a) 



ESRS only



Unselected

b) 



GRI only



Correct AnswerUnselected

c) 



ESRS and GRI only



Unselected

d) 



ESRS, GRI and IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards





Hide question 4 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is c.  



IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards are based on financial materiality, requiring reporting entities to consider the impact on the entity's financial prospects. GRI is based on impact materiality, requiring reporting entities to consider the impact of the business on the economy, environment and society. ESRS is based on double materiality ie both impact and financial materiality.

Entities using IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards to prepare sustainability-related disclosures are also required to use IFRS Accounting Standards to prepare financial statements.

Entities using IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards to prepare sustainability-related disclosures are required to use relevant SASB standards as a source of guidance.

Unselected

Correct AnswerUnselected

Unselected

Hide question 9 feedback

Feedback

The correct answer is c.



Statement 1 is false. IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards are designed to be compatible with IFRS Accounting Standards but entities using IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards are not required to use IFRS Accounting Standards.



Statement 2 is true. IFRS S1 specifies that entities must use the relevant SASB standard(s) for the industry/ies in which they operate as a source of guidance.
