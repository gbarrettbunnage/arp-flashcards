How this flashcard wizardry works

Quick controls + progress tracking info

🧩 Study a card

Each card is a practice question. Click it (or press Space) to reveal the answer, then mark whether you Got It or it Needs Review.

→ records Got It and advances, even before revealing the answer. Use Needs Review for an incorrect answer; the on-screen Skip button leaves it ungraded.

Confidence tracking

Select or change your 1-5 rating before or after revealing. The final rating is saved with your first Got It or Needs Review grade for that card in the session. Skipping does not save a rating. Confidence does not change mastery. Stats compares your ratings with your own grades; export/import includes rating history.

🎯 Learning & mastery

A card is only counted as mastered after you get it right on 2 separate sessions in a row. Get it right once and it sits in learning — it still shows in your normal sets and in the weak-area stats, just not yet as mastered, so you keep seeing it until the streak is complete. Marking it Needs Review resets the streak. You can change the threshold in Stats; set it to 1 if you'd rather a single correct answer count as mastered straight away.

Hide mastered is on by default and removes mastered cards from normal study and filtered printouts, not from your statistics. Switch it off to revise them. Explicit See also links can still open a mastered card.

⏱️ Exam practice

Exam mode times you per card. Stopwatch counts up (with an elapsed-per-mark pace); Countdown gives each card its marks × your target minutes per mark and turns red when you overrun. The target defaults to 1.8 min/mark and you can change it in the toolbar. The timer waits for you to press ▶ before it starts, and you can pause or reset it any time.

💾 Filters & saved progress

Use the filters to narrow by card type, module or provider, the toolbar to search, reorder the queue, or drill your weak cards. Your progress saves automatically in this browser. Because that save is per-browser, Export writes it to a file you can Import on another computer or browser — handy for moving between home and work, keeping a backup before clearing your browser, or carrying your streaks onto a newer version of this site.
